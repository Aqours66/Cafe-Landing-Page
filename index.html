Good to know

The questions we hear most at the counter.

Do you have free Wi-Fi?

Yes, free for everyone. Ask at the counter for the password.

Can I bring my laptop?

Of course. We have power sockets at most tables, and the quiet corner is great for work.

Do you have dairy-free milk?

Yes, oat and soy milk for a small extra charge.

Do I need to reserve a table?

Not usually, but it helps for groups of five or more and busy weekends.
